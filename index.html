Research

Research experience

The University of Chicago

Jun 2026 – Present
Research Intern · Knowledge Lab / Chicago, IL

I propose anchored exploration, a framework built on a two-layer phylogenetic network of 500,206 semiconductor designs spanning 1953 to 2024, inferring 493,053 ancestor–descendant relationships and 5,852 capability-transfer events from 563 specifications each. Alongside it I constructed and released a public dataset of 1.74M semiconductor products across 817 standardized attributes, covering 1930 to 2024. The network predicts product obsolescence at 0.72 AUC and capability origin at 0.74, beating the 0.62 achievable from a design’s own specifications.

Supervisor: Dr. Dawoon Jeong

Budapest University of Technology and Economics

Jan – May 2026
Visiting Student Researcher / Budapest, Hungary

I proposed SEED, a novel unsupervised method for meme-template classification that outperforms prior unsupervised baselines by 6% and matches CNN-level accuracy on the ImgFlip benchmark. Studying 53K+ posts across 1.5K+ templates, I cluster smoothed monthly usage trajectories to surface template lifecycles and long-run trends, and pair BERTopic with sentiment and emotion analysis over 79K+ posts to uncover how captions reshape a template’s meaning over time.

Supervisor: Dr. Roland Molontay · Mentor: Donat Koller

University of Illinois Chicago

Aug – Dec 2025
Research Intern / Chicago, IL

I developed MiCP, the first conformal-prediction framework with an adaptive rejection mechanism for multi-turn LLM reasoning, calibrating retrieval and prediction-set thresholds per reasoning step to give formal coverage across both retrieval and generation. Across five QA benchmarks, ReAct, and three LLMs it holds target coverage while cutting reasoning turns by up to 40.7% and prediction-set size by up to 22.1%.

Supervisor: Dr. Lu Cheng · Mentor: Xiaofan Zhou

NP-TaR

Oct – Dec 2025
NullPrompt-Trace and Reweight

An end-to-end robustness pipeline for decoder-only classifiers across AG News, HateXplain, and SST-2, combining null-input evaluation, Infini-gram corpus tracing for data reweighting, and post-fine-tuning analysis. I proposed NullBench to quantify default-class collapse through Default-Class Bias, Null Entropy, and Null Risk Score, and a trace-and-reweight mitigation that turns document-level label-pattern signals into weighted sampling manifests.

A multi-backend pipeline evaluating LLMs on skewed datasets (AG News, SST-2, HateXplain, MIMIC), with self-consistency and threshold or minority-first voting to curb label bias. Chicago Crime-Type Prediction

Multi-class classification over 1M+ incidents

A classifier for Chicago crime type over 1M+ incident records from 2020 to 2025, built on a reproducible Selenium-to-model pipeline spanning dashboard scraping, city-scale cleaning, and aggregate temporal feature engineering. I compared KNN, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost under a shared feature space, then paired the metrics with a threats-to-validity analysis and next steps in calibration and fairness-aware evaluation. Joint work with Anh Le for CSC-325.
